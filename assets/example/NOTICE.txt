The example book
================

happy_prince.json is the text of "The Happy Prince and Other Tales" by Oscar
Wilde (1888). The work is in the public domain in the United States (Wilde died
in 1900; the collection was published in 1888).

The text was taken from the Project Gutenberg eBook #902
(https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/902), proofread by David Price and Paul
Redmond, and cleaned for reading: the Gutenberg header and footer, the table of
contents, the dedication, the illustration markers and the hard line wraps were
removed, and each of the five stories is one chapter.

cover.jpg is the cover Project Gutenberg publishes for that ebook
(https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/902/pg902.cover.medium.jpg), a scan of the
1888 title page: "The Happy Prince and Other Tales, by Oscar Wilde, illustrated
by Walter Crane and Jacomb Hood". The title page is public domain along with the
text. It is 200x274, so it is honest at shelf size and soft full-screen; replace
it with a better scan if one turns up.
